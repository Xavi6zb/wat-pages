Overview

Wat!? is a casual party game built around quick rounds, voice recording and reversed audio. The app is intended to work without requiring you to create an account or provide personal profile information.

2

Microphone & audio

Wat!? may request access to your microphone so you can record audio during gameplay. Microphone access is used only when a feature requires it.

  • Audio is used to power the game experience, including playback and reversed playback.
  • Voice recordings are processed for gameplay on your device and are not uploaded to Wat!? servers.
  • Wat!? does not use your microphone in the background for advertising or profiling.
  • You can revoke microphone permission at any time in iOS Settings.
3

Personal data

Wat!? does not require an account, real name, phone number, address or contact list to play. If the app stores gameplay preferences or settings, they may be kept locally on your device so the experience works as expected.

4

Third-party services

If Wat!? uses Apple services required for distribution, purchases, crash reporting or other platform functionality, those services may process limited technical information under their own privacy policies. Wat!? does not sell personal information.

5

Children

Wat!? is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us unintentionally, contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

6

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Wat!? changes. The latest version will always be published on this page, with the revision date shown above.
